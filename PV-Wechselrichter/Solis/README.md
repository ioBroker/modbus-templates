Reference: Solis "RS485 MODBUS Communication Protocol (Hybrid)".

## Settings in ioBroker.modbus

- RS485 (Modbus RTU, 9600 8N1, device ID 1 by default) or a data logger with Modbus TCP.
- The addresses are the register numbers of the Solis protocol (e.g. 33079 active power, 33139 battery SOC). They are
  raw addresses; the import converts them to the "Use aliases" setting of the instance.
- All registers are polled (`poll = true`); deselect the ones you do not need before saving.
- Times of the charge/discharge slots (43143 ff.) are two registers each: `… hour` and `… minute`.
- Writing the holding registers (43xxx) changes the operation of the inverter, at your own risk.
